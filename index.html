01 / OUR GAME

Court is in session,

COURTROOM RPGIN DEVELOPMENTWINDOWS · MAC · IOS · ANDROID

Justice is Argued

You play Harold Beagll, a golden retriever attorney. Between trials you walk around Beagll Town, talking to suspects and digging up clues.

In court, every fight is a turn-based argument. Witnesses and prosecutors argue back. A good objection can wear them down or win over the jury, and a bad one leaves Harold tongue-tied.
